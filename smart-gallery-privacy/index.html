Smart Gallery — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Smart Gallery is a photo and video gallery for the pictures already on your phone. Your photos, videos and everything the app works out about them stay on your phone. None of them is ever uploaded.

The app is free and shows one banner ad from Google AdMob. That banner is the only reason the app uses the internet.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting. We cannot identify you, and we could not contact you unless you email us first. The ad banner is provided by Google; see Advertising below.

Advertising

The app shows a banner supplied by Google AdMob at the bottom of the gallery. It is removed while a photo or video is open full screen and while you edit. To show it, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us — we receive only anonymous, aggregated totals of how many ads were shown. Google's ad software has no access to your photos, videos or the text found in them. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later in the app's settings under Ad privacy choices. Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob help centre.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Your photos and videos

The app reads the photos and videos on your phone so it can show them, sort them into folders and dates, and let you search them. It changes, moves, copies or deletes a file only when you ask it to, and Android asks you to confirm before any file is moved, deleted or sent to the system Trash. Edits are always saved as a new copy; your original is left as it was.

Text and subject search, on your phone

If you choose to scan your pictures, the app reads the words in them (Latin and Devanagari scripts) and recognises general subjects such as "dog" or "document", so you can search for them. This runs entirely on your phone, using Google ML Kit models bundled inside the app. No picture and no recognised text ever leaves the phone. Pictures you have blurred with the app's PIN are never scanned.

What the app stores on your phone

  • your albums, favourites, protected photos and pinned folder covers
  • the words and subjects found by scanning, kept so search is fast
  • your settings, such as layout, theme and columns
  • for blurred pictures, a securely hashed four-digit PIN. The blur is an in-app display lock; it does not encrypt your files or hide them from other apps.

Android's cloud backup is turned off for this app, so none of this is copied to your Google account.

Backup files you make

You can export your albums and organisation to a file in a place you choose. That file holds file names, folder paths and the app's own organisation details. It contains no photos, no PIN and no recognised text. It is written only where you tell it to go, and we never see it.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos (read media images / video, or read storage on Android 12 and older): to show and organise your gallery. On Android 14 and newer you can allow only selected photos.
  • Internet and network state: for the ad banner only. Photos, videos and recognised text are never sent.
  • Advertising ID and Android's ad-services permissions: added by Google's ad software for the banner.
  • Run at startup, wake lock and foreground service: added by Android's WorkManager library, which the app uses to run a scan you started in the background and to resume it after a restart.

The app requests no location, no camera, no microphone and no contacts.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete it. Your photos and videos themselves are not removed by uninstalling. We hold nothing anywhere else to delete; for advertising data, reset your advertising ID as described above.

Children

Smart Gallery is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.
