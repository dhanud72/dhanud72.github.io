Pip's Wordwood — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026 (advertising banner added)

Pip's Wordwood is a word puzzle adventure. It has no account, no server and no analytics of our own. Every puzzle plays on your phone, with or without internet.

Your progress stays on your phone. The only data that goes anywhere is what Google's ad service needs to show the banner at the top of the screen.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and receive no data from the app. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting of our own. We cannot identify you, and we could not contact you unless you email us first.

What the app stores on your phone

To remember where you are in the game, the app keeps these in its own storage on your phone:

  • your progress: puzzles and stages cleared, stars, points and the words you have found
  • your practice records: daily puzzles, English Boost, Trail Trials and Pip's campfire reviews, including which answers were right the first time
  • story progress, keepsakes, the days you played, and any optional feedback you choose in a story ("Useful", "Too easy" or "Confusing")
  • your settings, such as sound and animations

None of it is uploaded by the app. Your story feedback stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows one banner at the top of the screen, supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version, and diagnostics about how the ad performed
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us. We receive only anonymous, aggregated totals of how many ads were shown. Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob help centre. In the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Daily reminder (optional)

If you turn it on, the app schedules a notification on your phone around the time you choose, telling you which land today's trail is in. The reminders are created and shown entirely on your phone by Android; nothing is sent anywhere. It is off until you say yes, and you can turn it off or change the time in the game's settings at any time.

Hearing words spoken

When you tap a button to hear a word or sentence, the app asks your phone's built-in text-to-speech service to read it aloud. It chooses an English voice that works without a network connection. The app never records or listens to your voice.

Sharing

Some screens offer to share a small result, such as a story challenge or a practice score. Sharing only happens when you tap the button. The app opens Android's share menu with the text shown, and you choose the app and the person it goes to. That app's own privacy policy then applies to what you send.

Android backup

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is made and kept by Android and your Google account, under your control, not by us.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked for only when you turn the daily reminder on. Refusing it is fine; the game works the same.
  • Run at startup (receive boot completed): lets your reminder schedule survive a phone restart.
  • Internet and network state: for the ad banner only. Puzzles never need the internet.
  • Advertising ID and Android's ad-services permissions, plus wake lock and foreground service: added by Google's ad software and the Android libraries it uses, for their own housekeeping. The game itself runs no foreground service.

The app requests no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts and no storage access.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete it all. There is nothing held anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Pip's Wordwood is meant for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. It has no chat, no sign-in and no way to share data with other players.
