DualCam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

DualCam is a video camera that records one take and saves it in both 16:9 and 9:16. It has no account, no server and no cloud storage. Recording, cropping and saving all happen on your phone.

Your videos and audio never leave your device. The only data that does is what Google's ad service needs to show a banner.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and hold no records about you. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting. We cannot identify you, and we could not contact you unless you email us first.

Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera to show the live preview and record video, and the microphone to record the sound of a take. It uses them only while the app is open on screen, and the microphone only while you are recording. You can turn audio off with the Audio button.

Nothing from the camera or microphone is streamed, uploaded, analysed or shared. There is no face detection or other recognition of any kind.

Your videos stay on your device

A take is first written to the app's own private storage, cropped to the other shape on the phone, and then saved to your gallery in Movies/DualCam. The private working copies are deleted as soon as the save completes.

The saved videos belong to you and are managed like any other video in your gallery. We never receive them, and the app does not upload or back them up anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows a banner supplied by Google AdMob. The banner is hidden while you are recording. To show it, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us — we receive only anonymous, aggregated totals of how many ads were shown. Google's ad software has no access to your camera, microphone or videos. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob help centre.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Permissions the app requests

  • CAMERA — to show the preview and record video.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — to record the sound of a take.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — for the ad banner only.
  • AD_ID, Android's ad-services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, _ATTRIBUTION, _TOPICS), WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE — added by Google's ad software and the Android libraries it uses, for their own housekeeping. The app itself runs no foreground or background service.

That is the complete list. Saving to the gallery needs no storage permission, and the app requests no location, no contacts and no access to your existing photos or videos.

Deleting your data

Delete any video from your gallery as usual. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes everything else it keeps. There is nothing held anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

DualCam is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
