Kundli Match — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Kundli Match compares two horoscopes for marriage (36 Gunas and 10 Porutham). It has no account, no server and no database. Every chart, score, dasha and wedding date is calculated on your phone.

Names and birth details never leave your device. The only data that does is what Google's ad service needs to show a banner, and the few look-ups described below, which carry no personal details.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and hold no records about you. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Birth details stay on your device

To match two people you enter names and their birth date, time and place (or only a star, or a name). The matches you save, your own kundli and the home town you pick are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded, not backed up by us and not shared. Swiping a saved match away deletes it; uninstalling the app removes everything.

The share card and the PDF report are made on your phone. They go only where you choose to send them.

Look-ups that use the internet

  • Place search: when a birthplace is not in the app's built-in list, the text you typed is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find it.
  • Village list (optional): if you choose to download it, the app fetches the public GeoNames file for India. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Explanations: when the built-in Learn section has no answer, the term you looked up is sent to Wikipedia.

None of these requests include names, birth dates or times, or any identifier from us. Like any web request they reveal your IP address to that service.

Advertising

The app shows one banner supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. See Google Privacy & Terms. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Permissions

The app itself asks only for INTERNET, for the banner and the look-ups above. Google's ad software adds its own housekeeping permissions (AD_ID, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and Android's ad-services permissions). The app requests no location, camera, contacts, microphone or storage permission.

Children

Kundli Match is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13.
