Banner March — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Banner March is an offline game: lead a hero and an army down the road, grow it through gates and fell the giants. It has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.

The app does not ask for internet access, so nothing you do in the game ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and hold no records about you or anyone else. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.

What is stored on your device

Your coins, heroes, upgrades, best scores, the tips you have already seen and the sound on/off setting are kept in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not uploaded or shared. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Permissions

The app asks only for VIBRATE. It requests no internet, location, camera, contacts, microphone or storage permission.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If a later version changes any of this (for example by adding ads), this page will be updated with a new date at the top before that version is released.
