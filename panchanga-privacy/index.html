Panchanga — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Panchanga is a Hindu calendar. It has no account, no server and no database. The panchang, muhurtas, horoscope and kundli are all calculated on your phone from astronomy.

Your location and your birth details never leave your device. The only data that does is what Google's ad service needs to show a banner, and the name of a panchang term when the app looks up its meaning on Wikipedia.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and hold no records about you. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting. We cannot identify you, and we could not contact you unless you email us first.

Your location stays on your device

The panchang depends on where you are, because sunrise does. You can choose your city from a list built into the app — searching it works offline and sends nothing anywhere.

If you tap the location button instead, the app asks Android for your approximate location (city-level, not GPS precision), takes one reading, and uses it to calculate the panchang. It does not track you: there is no background location and no repeated reading. The place you choose is saved only in the app's own storage on your phone, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your birth details stay on your device

To draw a kundli, you enter a birth date, time, place and time zone. The chart is calculated on the phone. If you save it, the details are kept in the app's own storage on your phone so the chart is there next time — they are not uploaded, not backed up by us and not shared. The Delete saved kundli button removes them at once.

Looking up the meaning of terms

Beside some words — Rahu Kaal, a nakshatra, a festival — an ⓘ explains what it means. That explanation comes from Wikipedia and Wikidata: the app asks them for the article on that fixed term and keeps the answer on the phone for 90 days.

These requests contain only the name of the term — never your location, your birth details or anything about you. Like any web request they reveal your IP address to the Wikimedia Foundation, whose handling of it is described in the Wikimedia privacy policy.

Advertising

The app shows a banner supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us — we receive only anonymous, aggregated totals of how many ads were shown. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob help centre.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Permissions the app requests

  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — only when you tap the location button, to find your approximate position for sunrise. Refusing it is fine: choose your city from the list instead.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — for the ad banner and the Wikipedia look-ups described above.
  • AD_ID, Android's ad-services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, _ATTRIBUTION, _TOPICS), WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE — added by Google's ad software and the Android libraries it uses, for their own housekeeping. The app itself runs no foreground or background service.

That is the complete list. The app requests no precise location, no background location, no camera, no contacts, no microphone and no storage permission.

Deleting your data

Everything the app keeps is on your phone. Delete a saved kundli with the button on the Kundli screen, or remove all of the app's data by clearing its storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. There is nothing held anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Panchanga is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
