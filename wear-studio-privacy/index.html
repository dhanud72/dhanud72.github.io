Wear Studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Wear Studio is a garment mockup tool. It has no account, no server and no database. The artwork you put on a garment is read from your device, used to draw the mockup, and never sent anywhere.

The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's ad service needs to show a banner. Everything else stays on the device.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and hold no records about you. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting. We cannot identify you, and we could not contact you unless you email us first.

Your images stay on your device

When you add artwork, Android's own photo picker hands the app a single image you chose. The app reads it, resizes it, and draws it onto the 3D garment. That image is not uploaded, not backed up and not shared with us or anyone else. The picker's temporary copy is deleted as soon as the image has been read.

Because the system photo picker is used, the app never asks for access to your photo library. It only ever sees the one image you hand it, and it holds no permission to look at the rest.

Mockups you export as JPG, GIF or MP4 are written to your own device gallery, in an album called Wear Studio. They stay there until you delete them. We never see them.

Advertising

The app shows a banner supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us — we receive only anonymous, aggregated totals of how many ads were shown. Google's handling of it is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob help centre.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Permissions the app requests

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load the ad banner. The app itself makes no other network requests; the 3D viewer runs entirely from files inside the app.
  • AD_ID and the related advertising permissions — required by Google's ad software.

That is the complete list. The app requests no camera, no location, no contacts, no microphone and no storage permission.

Children

Wear Studio is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
