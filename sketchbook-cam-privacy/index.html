Sketchbook Cam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Sketchbook Cam is a camera that turns what it sees into a live drawing (pencil, charcoal, ink, paint and toon styles) as you film or take photos. It has no account, no server and no cloud storage. Every style is drawn by your phone's own graphics chip, on the device.

Your photos, videos and audio never leave your device unless you share them yourself. The only data that does is what Google's ad service needs to show a banner.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no servers and hold no records about you. There is no sign-in, no profile, no analytics and no crash reporting. We cannot identify you, and we could not contact you unless you email us first.

Camera and microphone

The app uses the camera to show the live drawn preview and to take photos and videos, and the microphone only while you are recording a video, to record its sound. It uses them only while the app is open on screen.

Nothing from the camera or microphone is streamed, uploaded or shared. The drawing effects work on the picture as a whole; there is no face recognition, no identification of people and no AI service of any kind.

Photos you choose to convert

You can pick a photo from your gallery to turn into a drawing. This uses Android's own photo picker, so the app sees only the one photo you pick, and has no access to the rest of your gallery. The drawing is made on the phone and saved as a new picture; your original is not changed.

Your pictures stay on your device

Photos are saved to your gallery in Pictures/Sketchbook Cam and videos in Movies/Sketchbook Cam. They belong to you and are managed like any other picture in your gallery. We never receive them, and the app does not upload or back them up anywhere.

Saved pictures carry a small "Sketchbook Cam" watermark in one corner. It is only text drawn into the image and contains no hidden information about you or your device.

The Share button opens Android's share sheet. A picture goes only to the app you choose there, at your request.

Your choices in the app (style, timer, paper and similar settings) are stored on the phone only.

Advertising

The app shows one banner, supplied by Google AdMob, in a strip below the camera controls. It is never part of your photos or videos. To show it, Google's ad software collects information from the device, which typically includes:

  • the device's advertising ID
  • IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, OS version and app version
  • interactions with the ad, such as whether it was seen or tapped

This is collected by Google, not by us; we receive only anonymous, aggregated totals of how many ads were shown. Google's ad software has no access to your camera, microphone, photos or videos. In the European Economic Area and the UK the app asks for your consent, using Google's consent form, before ads are personalised. Google's handling of the data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob help centre.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Permissions the app requests

  • CAMERA — to show the drawn preview and take photos and videos.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — to record the sound of a video.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — for the ad banner only.
  • AD_ID, Android's ad-services permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, _ATTRIBUTION, _TOPICS), WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE — added by Google's ad software and the Android libraries it uses, for their own housekeeping. The app itself runs no foreground or background service.

That is the complete list. Saving to the gallery needs no storage permission, and the app requests no location, no contacts and no access to your existing photos or videos beyond the single photo you pick yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete any picture from your gallery as usual. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes your settings. There is nothing held anywhere else for us to delete.

Children

Sketchbook Cam is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
