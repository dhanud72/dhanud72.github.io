Beat Audiobook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Beat Audiobook is an offline audiobook player. Your books, your listening position and your listening history stay on your device. We do not run accounts, we do not have a server, and we cannot see your library.

This policy explains the few things that do leave your phone, and what controls you have over them.

What stays on your device

Everything that makes the app work is stored locally, in the app's own private storage, and is deleted when you uninstall:

  • Your library — the audiobooks found on your device: titles, authors, cover art, chapter lists and file locations.
  • Your position in every book — the exact place you stopped, per book.
  • Bookmarks and notes you create.
  • Listening statistics — time listened, streaks, daily goal progress.
  • Your settings — theme, playback speed, skip and rewind amounts.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. The app never uploads your audio files, and never uploads a list of what you own.

What the app accesses on your device

  • Audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) — needed to find and play audiobooks already stored on your phone. The app reads these files; it does not modify, move or delete them, and does not copy them anywhere.

What leaves your device

1. Advertising

The app shows banner adverts through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information, including your advertising ID, IP address and general usage data, to serve and measure adverts. This happens under Google's own privacy policy, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

We do not send Google your library, your books, your positions or your notes.

2. Optional online title look-up — off by default

If you turn on Settings → Library → Identify online, the app sends a book title (text only) to public catalogue search APIs to work out whether something is an audiobook or music, and to correct its spelling:

  • Apple iTunes Search API — itunes.apple.com
  • Deezer API — api.deezer.com

Only the title text is sent. No file, no account, no device identifier, and no list of your library. Answers are cached on your device so the same title is never looked up twice. This setting is off unless you turn it on, and you can clear the cached results at any time from Settings.

3. Optional speech model download

If you use Name chapters or Generate subtitles, the app downloads a speech-recognition model (about 75 MB) once. The download is a plain file fetch. All speech recognition then runs on your device — your audio is never uploaded.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your controls

  • Adverts: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Online look-up: turn off, and clear cached results, in the app's Settings.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the app removes all local data.
